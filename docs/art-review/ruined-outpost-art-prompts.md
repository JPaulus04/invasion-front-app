# Ruined Outpost candidate generation specification

Built-in image_gen was used. Source IDs and normalization are in ruined-outpost-generated-sources.json. PNGs are installed under OutpostZero/Assets.xcassets/world1_ruined_outpost_*.imageset. These are candidate game assets, not evidence of runtime visual acceptance.

## Floors
Ground-only portrait 1024x1536; flat orthographic overhead camera; polished dimensional cartoon mobile style; soft upper-left light; moss-green grass, warm earth, sparse flush masonry and leaf litter; quiet open central 75%; no elevated props, characters, water, portals, text, HUD, cast shadows or vignette. First composition uses broken edge stone impressions suggesting loose alternate upward approaches without a literal road. Upper 20% and lower 15% stay quiet. Edges requested to match grassy surroundings, but seamless tiling has not been validated.

Variants two and three use floor one as the style reference. Two concentrates flush fragments at lower left/upper right with quiet central grass; three uses upper left/lower right fragments and perimeter earth patches. Both request fewer leaf speckles for bullet readability. Independent generated compositions, not mirrored duplicates.

## Shared prop prompt
Use case: stylized-concept. Production isolated game sprite for Outpost Zero Ruined Outpost. ONE object centered fully visible with 12% transparent margin. Polished dimensional cartoon 3D mobile action game asset, chunky readable forms at 50 pixels tall, elevated orthographic camera looking downward 45 degrees, front and top visible, soft upper-left lighting. Subtle bevels, saturated but natural colors. True transparent alpha background. No ground plane, cast shadow outside object, surrounding scene, text, UI, watermark or frame. Clear lower contact point for runtime shadow. Square image requested.

## Prop subjects
- Wall: short broken ancient outpost wall, chunky cool-gray stone blocks, irregular fractured top, moss, three blocks wide/two tall, low silhouette.
- Boulder: low rounded cluster of three weathered gray rocks with small moss patches and broad stable base; no tall spire.
- Vegetation: compact rounded emerald fern and broadleaf cluster, low grounded bush; no flowers or trunk.
- Debris: small cluster of three to five broken angular stone masonry pieces with restrained moss.
- Lantern: squat stone pedestal and small enclosed amber lantern with sturdy dark metal casing; restrained light, no broad glow.
- Crate: squat closed weathered wooden supply crate, dark iron corner bands, warm brown wood and small moss patch; no logos.
